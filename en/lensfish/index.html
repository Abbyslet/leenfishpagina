LeensFish

Offensive Cybersecurity Program

LeensFish is a distinctive mark of advanced cybersecurity, designed to identify digital assets that meet technical protection, defense, and resilience criteria against cyber threats.

Our application on servers, applications, platforms, and enterprise infrastructure represents the adoption of a set of security measures, subjected to technical evaluation and verification processes, aimed at ensuring a level of protection in accordance with the standards established by LeensFish.

Beyond the conventional capabilities of a firewall The document outlines a multi-dimensional defense architecture that integrates prevention, detection, and response mechanisms against threats, behavioral analysis, continuous monitoring, vulnerability assessment, and containment measures against security incidents.
